## Test 1: Multi-Resolution Harmonics — BREADCRUMB

**Script:** `scripts/multi_resolution_probe.py` (committed)
**Hypothesis:** Vedic Varga tradition encodes meaningful structure at multiple resolutions (D9, D27, D60). Extending our harmonic sweep from n∈{1..12} to n∈{1..12, 16, 20, 24, 27, 36, 60} should reveal structure we're missing.

**Result:**
- Arithmetic had MORE extended-harmonic structure than grammar (23.9% vs 8.4% at L0, 26.5% vs 0.9% at L4) — backwards from expected
- Most extended-harmonic signal is embedding grid mathematics (bands 10,19 at n=60, MRL=0.995, appears in BOTH models — grid artifact from m1=5 × 12 = 60)
- Grammar L0 grid-2 bands 56-78: 8-pair cluster at n=9 (Navamsa), MRL 0.75-0.78 vs standard 0.53-0.58, ~40% improvement, NOT present in arithmetic

**Status:** Open breadcrumb. The n=9 cluster is not pure grid arithmetic because it's grammar-specific. Confounded by training stage (80K undertrained) and capacity (168-dim ~94% full). Mechanism: grid-2 m2=7 × Navamsa 9-fold division creates interaction positions. Needs re-test on converged larger model or BPE checkpoint.

**Lesson learned:** Opus initially framed this as dismissive. Marco caught the framing drift: "the model is barely learned at 80K, 168-dim has no freedom." The breadcrumb was real and under-weighted. ## Test 2: Context/Dignity — CONFIRMED

**Script:** `scripts/context_dignity_probe.py` (committed)
**Hypothesis:** Hellenistic Essential Dignities describe context-dependent strength. Wave-engine analog: a token's processing (cos, energy) should change with surrounding tokens.

**Result (clean confirmation on first test):**
- **'e':** solo L3 cos=0.46, in "sentence" 0.26, in "e." 0.05. Shifts of 0.20 and 0.41. 'e' in "he" matches solo (shift 0.02 — learned bigram)
- **'a':** solo L2 cos=0.24, in "an" 0.61. Shift 0.37, 2.5× more structure preserved in the determiner unit
- **'.':** directional dignity. ".A" shifts L3 by 0.12 (sentence-start). "t.", "n.", "e." all shift by only 0.03. Cares about what follows, not what precedes
- **'s':** lowest dignity effect (max 0.13). Context-stable. The most phase-distinctive token is also the most dignity-independent

**The phase-dignity correlation:** Phase-distinctive tokens are dignity-independent. Phase-generic tokens are dignity-dependent. Two measurements of the same underlying fact — tokens with context-free structural roles are both placed at distinctive angles AND processed consistently.

**Status:** CONFIRMED as a measurable phenomenon. Directly maps to Hellenistic Domicile/Exaltation/Peregrine. 's' is "in Domicile everywhere."

## Test 3: Grid Encoding — CONFIRMED, Surprising Direction

**Script:** `scripts/grid_encoding_probe.py` (committed)
**Hypothesis:** Off-grid positions (unreachable by any real token) have no learned processing, should pass through the model with minimal change.

**Result (opposite of pattern-matcher behaviour):**
- **L3 cos: on-grid = 0.168, off-grid = 0.301**
- Off-grid positions preserved 80% MORE than on-grid
- The model destroys what it recognises, preserves what it doesn't
- Grid-1 vs Grid-2 similar (0.162 vs 0.204) — the n=9 cluster is fine structure, not grid-level bulk effect

**Status:** CONFIRMED. Completes the L3 regime shift story — destruction is targeted at recognised inputs, not general. Consistent with GPT-2 comparison (language needs destruction) and chaos theory framing (controlled destruction past bifurcation).

## Synthesis Written: The Targeted Destruction Pattern

Tests 2 and 3 independently converged on the same mechanism from different directions:

- **Dignity:** model destroys familiar contexts more than unfamiliar (recognition triggers processing)
- **Grid encoding:** model destroys on-grid positions more than off-grid (recognition triggers processing)

Same mechanism, two domains. L3 learned not just *how much* to destroy but *where to aim*. Trigger is recognition.

**Working hypothesis (not a finding yet):** The catalog's abstract concepts are organised by *when* in a model's development they manifest. Context-sensitive concepts (dignity, direction) show up early because they're how the model learns to use what it has. Context-independent concepts (multi-resolution) show up later because they're how the model organises what it has learned. Needs testing across training stages and dimensions.
